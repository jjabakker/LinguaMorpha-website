# Follow LinguaMorpha

LinguaMorpha doesn't market like a typical app. There are no daily tips, no streak nags, no engagement-bait threads. Social channels exist for one job: **letting interested people know when something ships**. That's it.

If you want a quiet, low-volume way to hear about new modules (French in v1.2, Italian in v1.4, Portuguese in v1.6), feature updates, and the occasional grammar-coverage milestone — pick whichever of the channels below fits how you already use the internet.

---

## The most important channel: the App Store itself

If you have LinguaMorpha installed and your App Store **automatic updates** are on, you'll get every new version delivered without doing anything. That's the channel we actually optimise for — the release notes attached to each app-version update are the canonical source of truth for what's new.

Open **Settings → App Store → App Updates** on your iOS device to confirm automatic updates are enabled. The toggle is on by default, but worth checking.

## What you won't find us on

A few platforms we considered and chose not to use, transparency-style:

### Instagram, TikTok, YouTube

These platforms reward daily content marketing — word-of-the-day reels, grammar-explainer videos, etymology-thread carousels. That kind of work would be a full-time job, and a Latin-grammar app that bills itself as the antidote to streak-engagement-bait apps shouldn't be playing that game itself. We may revisit later if there's a niche fit, but not at v1.0.

### Facebook

Not a fit for the audience (adult self-directed learners, classics students, language hobbyists). We considered a Facebook page for completeness and decided against the maintenance overhead.

### A daily newsletter

We considered an opt-in email newsletter and decided the App Store + Mastodon + Bluesky combination covers the same job without asking anyone for an email address. We also don't run analytics, and a newsletter would require either a hosted service (analytics implied) or self-hosting (operational burden) — neither fit the no-accounts, no-tracking brand.
