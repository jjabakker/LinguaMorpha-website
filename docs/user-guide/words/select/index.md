# Select Words

The Select Words screen lets you narrow down the Word pool to the set you want to practise. The screen shows a live count of how many words match your current filters.

---

## Filters

- **CEFR Level** — A1 (Beginner) through C2 (Mastery).
- **Frequency** — Very Common, Common, or Less Common.
- **Grammatical Category** — adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, numbers, …
- **Topic** — limit to one or more thematic categories (e.g. time, body, work, travel).

Tap **Matching words** to browse the full list before confirming the selection.

---

## What's in the Words set?

Everything that's a single word but isn't a verb or a noun:

- **Adjectives** — masculine and feminine forms are merged into one entry. The flashcard shows the form appropriate to the context, so you encounter both forms across sessions.
- **Adverbs** — *siempre*, *nunca*, *muy*, *bien*, *mal*, *despacio*, *demasiado*, …
- **Prepositions** — *para*, *por*, *con*, *sin*, *desde*, *hasta*, …
- **Conjunctions** — *aunque*, *pero*, *porque*, *mientras*, …
- **Numbers** — cardinal numbers, ordinals, fractions.

For multi-word expressions, see the [Phrases](../../phrases/) section.

---

## Word pool vs session size

These are two separate things:

- Your **word pool** is the set of words eligible to appear in any session — the result of your filters.
- Your **session size** (set in [Settings → Sessions](../../settings/#sessions)) is how many of those words appear in a single sitting (10, 20, 30, 50, or All).

Each session, the app picks the best N words from your pool, prioritising unseen and weak ones.

---

## Tips

- The **Grammatical Category** filter is the most useful one here. Drilling all prepositions together, or all adverbs together, builds intuition faster than mixing them.
- Numbers (cardinals, ordinals, fractions) deserve their own focused sessions — filter to just numbers and run a short drill until they're automatic.
