# Words Coach

The Words section covers **single-word vocabulary that isn't a verb or a noun**: adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, numbers, and other isolated words.

Examples: *siempre*, *nunca*, *muy*, *bien*, *despacio*, *para*, *por*, *aunque*, *grande*, *pequeño*, *primero*.

It runs the same three-step flow as Verbs and Nouns: **Setup**, **Study**, **Test**.

---

## Setup

- [Select Words](select/) — filter by CEFR level, frequency, grammatical category, and topic.

## Study

- [Browse Words](study/) — a searchable list of the words in your current selection, with translations and audio.

## Test

- [Word Meanings](test/) — flashcards in either direction (Spanish ↔ your language), or a random mix.

---

## When to use Words

The Words section captures the **connective tissue** of everyday Spanish: the adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, and conjunctions that hold sentences together but aren't covered by the structured Verbs or Nouns sections.

For multi-word expressions like *buenos días*, *de vez en cuando*, *encantado*, see the [Phrases](../phrases/) section instead.
