# Browse Words

The Browse screen for Words is a passive reference: no testing, no answers recorded. Use it to look up a word, refresh your memory before a session, or scan through what's in your current selection.

---

## What you see

A searchable list of every word in your current selection, showing:

- The Spanish word
- The English (or Dutch) translation
- A small badge for the CEFR level
- A speaker icon — tap to hear the word spoken aloud

Tap any word to open its detail view, where you can see additional context including the grammatical category, topic tags, and usage notes.

---

## When to use Browse vs Test

- **Browse** is passive — read, listen, refresh. Nothing is recorded.
- [**Word Meanings**](../test/) is active — flashcards record your answers and feed the adaptive engine.

A common workflow is to spend a minute in Browse scanning the set you're about to drill, then jump straight into the test.
